# Job Tracker

Default workflow: inspect first -> analyze JD -> score -> decide -> tailor only if worth it -> update tracker -> stop.

## Search Focus

- Base location: Bukit Jelutong, Shah Alam, Selangor.
- Primary market: Selangor first.
- Preferred commute: 10-15km from Bukit Jelutong.
- Quality rule: apply only to roles with clear fit, fresh JD, practical commute, and truthful resume evidence.
- Strong nearby targets: Shah Alam, Glenmarie, Subang Jaya, Ara Damansara, Kelana Jaya, Petaling Jaya, Damansara, Kota Damansara, Setia Alam.
- Secondary targets: Kuala Lumpur only if hybrid/remote, salary/value is strong, and commute burden is justified.
- Default skip: vague JD, unknown company/client, far onsite role, weak salary, seniority mismatch, or low ATS fit.

## Decision Gates

- `Strong Apply`: 90+ overall, fresh JD, 80+ ATS score, 10-15km or remote/hybrid, and clear Master Resume evidence.
- `Apply`: 80-89 overall, practical commute, clear JD, and no major seniority mismatch.
- `Stretch Apply`: 70-79 overall only when role is high-value and gaps are explainable.
- `Skip`: below 70, far onsite, vague JD, weak salary/value, low ATS fit, or seniority beyond current evidence.
- Resume tailoring is allowed only for `Strong Apply` or `Apply`.

## Allowed Values

- Company Type: `MNC`, `SME`, `Startup`, `Agency`, `GLC`, `Other`
- Work Mode: `Onsite`, `Hybrid`, `Remote`
- Source: `Indeed`, `LinkedIn`, `JobStreet`, `Company Site`, `Referral`, `Other`
- Job Freshness: `Today`, `1-3 days`, `4-7 days`, `8-14 days`, `15+ days`
- Seniority: `Entry`, `Junior`, `Mid`, `Senior`, `Lead`
- Decision: `Strong Apply`, `Apply`, `Stretch Apply`, `Skip`
- Status: `Found`, `Reviewed`, `Resume Tailored`, `Applied`, `Follow-up`, `Interview`, `Rejected`, `Archived`

## Before Applying

1. Read full JD.
2. Extract job details.
3. Classify company type.
4. Estimate distance KM from Bukit Jelutong if location exists.
5. Identify seniority.
6. Check salary/value.
7. Extract ATS keywords.
8. Compare with [[Master Resume]].
9. Identify gaps.
10. Score suitability.
11. Decide Apply or Skip.
12. Tailor resume only if `Strong Apply` or `Apply`.
13. Never fabricate anything.
14. Update this tracker.
